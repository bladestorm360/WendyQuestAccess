# Entity soundscape

Open **F8 → Audio → Soundscape** to turn on nearby entity cues. Soundscape defaults off and its overall volume starts at 45 percent. People and merchants, Exits, Treasure, Interactible objects and Checkpoints each have their own toggle and volume. Categories start on at 100 percent of the overall soundscape volume. Choose a category to preview its cue, including separate door and stairs previews under Exits. A category preview works while that category or Soundscape is off, as long as Mod sounds is on and the effective volume is above zero. Back returns focus to the category or Soundscape button. These controls use the existing keyboard and controller settings input. [Audio and output controls](audio.md)

## Native presentation and entity eligibility

Use the same active event/page and target classification as the [field browser](../src/world/snapshot.js). Resolve the presentation separately for ambient cues. The field browser can identify authored navigation targets under the pathfinding naming exception. Ambient cues require evidence that the native map currently presents the source. Existing route and puzzle guidance retain their own rules. Treasure cues identify the category only.

| Category | Verified producer | Connected eligibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| People and merchants | `Spriteset_Map.createCharacters` builds the stock sprite list from the active native event objects. `Sprite_Character.update` updates its bitmap, frame, position, opacity and visibility. | Use the live sprite's identity, visibility, opacity, current frame and viewport position. An empty, transparent, erased, collected or inactive page does not qualify. Use the native interpolated location for moving actors. Most exit pages have no event sprite. | Use a generic exit for a verified exposed boundary, trail, arch or marked transfer. Assign a door or stair cue only when the current art proves the subtype. | Checkpoints | Map 196 event 21 and Map 200 events 6 and 15 invoke the native checkpoint light and request autosave. All three have the same visibly authored platform. | Identify those three sites from the active event plus the current platform tiles. Checkpoints retain their existing Objects navigation and puzzle feedback. Background autosave triggers, forced saves and ordinary menu saves require no ambient emitter. |
| Field items | The actionable treasure scan found authored chests and openable barrels. Other nonchest rewards were dialogue or script interactions. The standalone treasure marker found in the asset scan belongs to a developer area. | No separate field pickup cue was established. Keep reward identity out of ambient output. Among 937 actionable pages with a direct native transfer, only 11 declare an event graphic. No map-page or common-event command opens the stock save screen. Ten map pages request autosave directly. The three checkpoint pages use the same tileset 8 layer pair, tile 680 on layer 2 and tile 529 on layer 3 at the event cell. The other seven calls are progression triggers, including a parallel page. ## Cue scheduling and cancellation

[The scheduler](../src/world/soundscape.js) runs from the existing settled field update in `MapScene.js` at original byte 62779. It reads one bounded sprite and event list in postrender when the next cue is due. Eligible sources share one staggered output lane. It visits up to 520 sprite entries and 512 event entries and inspects at most 1,024 commands on a nearby page. The renderer matches live event and sprite identities and checks the current texture, alpha, viewport position and relevant tile values. The same active target producer serves navigation and soundscape. Map-relative interpolation and wraparound deltas determine an absolute screen bearing. Turning in place leaves the bearing unchanged. The six-tile ceiling can shrink to the native player light radius with a 16-pixel margin. The radius is read from the installed lighting plugin's current state, whose getter starts at original byte 121144 in `Community_Lighting_MZ.js`. The renderer submits only the cue, bearing and gain to the existing worker. [Presentation adapter](../src/world/soundscape-presentation.js) · [Shared target producer](../src/world/snapshot.js)

The scheduler chooses the least recently heard eligible source across all enabled categories and favors the nearer one when tied. Adjacent tiles with the same named exit and cue type share a turn. The closest real tile supplies the bearing. Distance lowers gain. The 95 ms sample and spatial tail are followed by a short gap between sources. At 60 FPS the eight-frame spacing is about 133 ms, with at least 26 frames before a source repeats. Beacon and cave timing take precedence when their shared channel is in use. A current sample retires when its actor, page or artwork changes, or its relative position drifts by more than half a tile. Settings, focus loss, native accepted event entry, transfer, idle, resize and shutdown retire the owning generation. The shared SDL callback checks that generation before submitting more PCM. [Native transition and audio ownership](audio.md#ownership-and-cancellation)

Seven original procedural sounds go through the same HRTF and stereo paths. People have a low rising pair, exits a falling pair, doors a low double knock, stairs three rising tones, treasure a bright pair, objects a single low tone and checkpoints three tones with a dip in the middle. The current definitions are working effects. A replacement goes in the cue bank and retains its cue ID and both spatial output modes. [Cue bank](../src/services/windows/spatial-cue.hpp)

## Offline verification and remaining coverage

The installed-method fixture verified a moving NPC, rotation, category order, live treasure collection, missing/offscreen texture, native lighting range, visible doors and stairs, an ordinary edge exit, the checkpoint platform and accepted event cancellation. It independently decoded all 25 native tile edits at the 24 world entrances and verified that original terrain was quiet and each painted marker qualified. The matching NW.js 0.48.4 fixture submitted an ambient object cue to silent SDL, previewed a door, checked focus return and restart persistence, and retired the source on the event boundary. The native build verified all seven generated waveforms, HRTF lateral placement and lower rear pitch. [Installed-method fixture](../tests/soundscape.cjs) · [Matching NW fixture](../tests/nw-smoke.py)

The throttled Map 41 fixture measured 0.07 ms total over 260 frames with soundscape off and 2.45 ms on, with a 0.28 ms maximum sampled frame. The serial and parallel fixture runs reported similar soundscape results. One earlier serial run recorded 37.22 ms over an eight-step beacon loop. Adjacent runs recorded 1.44 ms and 1.38 ms on that loop with silent requests. The fixtures use synthetic rendering and device timing.
