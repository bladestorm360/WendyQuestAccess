# On-map quest tracker

When the tracker shows a quest, the mod speaks the displayed title, when available, and the next unfinished objective with its displayed count. In **Speech**, enable **Autodetail** to hear the visible objective list after that announcement. **Menu indexing** adds the line position and count when reviewing. Both settings default off.

Stand still on the map and press **I** to read the current tracker. **Shift+Up** and **Shift+Down** move through the lines. **Shift+Left** and **Shift+Right** jump to the first and last line. On an Xbox layout, press and release **View**, then **Y** to read details or use the D-pad in mod mode to review. The controller returns to ordinary input after the action. The controls can be remapped. An existing custom binding can move or leave a new quest binding unassigned.

The small tracker shows objectives. An expanded tracker shows the title too. Once the title has been shown for a quest, it stays in review when that quest collapses in the same tracker window. A new quest starts review at its first line. During native dialogue, use the dialogue's own repeat command. Quest controls become available when the field resumes.

## Installed producers and ownership

The installed game has its library journal menu command and shortcut disabled. The observed game route is the tracker created by `Scene_Map.createQuestNavigation`, which adds a `Window_QuestMapNavigation` to the native window layer. The game sets its navigation quest, chooses whether to expand it, and moves the tracker offscreen when hidden. [Quest entry audit](menus.md#quest-entry-and-tracker-trace)

On Steam build 26578620 the fingerprinted quest plugin paints the tracker in `drawNavContents` at original byte 157497. The transform observes settled paints at 157600–158215. A short-lived wrapper on that window's own bitmap records the title and native objective rows at their draw calls. The native root-quest painter draws completed rows and the first incomplete row. Other quest types draw all rows selected by their native painter. The game owns clear-note and counter visibility. The mod uses the actual draws and their geometry and applies no rules based on unrevealed objectives or quest names. [Registration transform](../tools/registration.py) · [Paint observer](../src/menus/quest-paint.js)

The tracker stages at most 24 text draws of at most 96 characters from a paint. It restores the bitmap method at paint exit, including on a native exception. After the settled map update at byte 369098, one removable postrender listener checks that the same window, contents, revision and quest are visible. The observer checks the client area and viewport. The current review holds up to eight lines. Repeated equal-text rows stay in order. Native semantic refresh at 375124 revokes the previous page before repaint. A fold revokes speech during motion and preserves the same quest's review position. It can announce a title on its first settled reveal.

The field and tracker retain native references on the renderer. The speech service receives only bounded output records. Hiding, focus loss, resize, settings, events and scene departure revoke current output. The tracker can capture a native paint during a settled visible dialogue wait, then publish only when the field is ready. Transfer, fade, save, resource and event-entry gates prevent tracker paint sampling in protected phases. ## Offline evidence and next checks

The installed-method fixture loads the fingerprinted tracker paint, refresh and scene update methods and stock MZ text processing into memory. It confirms the initial incomplete row, later reveal, clear note, expanded title, fold cursor, repeated rows, clipping, speech retirement, listener replacement and bitmap restoration. The bitmap, viewport and field state are fixture stand-ins. In two runs, 400 stable synthetic frames took 0.36–0.54 ms with the observer bypassed and 1.30–1.39 ms connected, under the enforced runner. The matching NW.js fixture verifies the packaged headline, Autodetail, keyboard **I**, mod-mode controller **Y** and handback when hidden. These timings do not measure game or audible responsiveness. [Installed-method fixture](../tests/quest.test.cjs) · [Matching NW.js fixture](../tests/nw-smoke.py)

A tracker that has already finished its only paint behind a native gate needs a later eligible paint before this observer can read it. In particular, native destruction preserves the target fold state in `Game_Temp`, and a replacement window created in the small state has no automatic collapse to trigger another paint. Trace a save restore, transfer with a collapsed tracker, and a show after a hidden refresh. Close that gap through a bounded read-only path at a settled native boundary before final handoff. Allow 2–4 engineering hours for restoration and the exact lifecycle fixture. Allow another 2–4 hours within the existing quest and world estimates to qualify actual event and map destinations, including gated links and unavailable targets.

For the final player pass, accept the first quest and wait for the field to return. Expect a current objective and a count where shown. Press **I** and review the last line. Advance the objective using ordinary game actions, then repeat. Expect the next objective only after native reveal. Repeat after a transfer and after continuing a save with an active quest.
