# Route beacon

Turn **Navigation beacon** on under **F8 → Audio**, or use **Shift+B** on a settled walking map or aboard a settled vehicle. It defaults off. Its volume defaults to 65 percent and changes in ten-point increments. Preview the forward pulse, turn cue or arrival cue in the same group. All three use the saved beacon volume and the shared sound and HRTF switches. **Shift+H** independently toggles wall sounds.

Select a destination and request directions with **P** in the Letters layout, **Backslash** in Brackets and backslash, or the configured controller directions control. Speech gives the route according to the current navigation settings. The beacon uses the same course and gives a short pulse from the next verified direction. A faster, stronger pulse means the next turn or destination is nearer. A double note asks for a turn. A rising pair signals the verified interaction approach and ends the route. Use ordinary native movement and the action button to interact. **Shift+V** stops current navigation. Browsing to another destination, opening a menu, leaving the map, losing focus or starting an event stops the current beacon. [Autowalk and stop controls](navigation.md#native-input-boundary-for-autowalk)

For the default controller bindings, press **View/Select**, release it, then press **LT** to toggle the beacon, move the **right stick right** to toggle wall sounds, or move the **right stick left** to stop current navigation. On a settled field without mod mode, LT requests directions and the right stick browses. Both keyboard layouts and Controller bindings support separate remaps. Existing valid keyboard and controller assignments keep precedence over the new defaults. The L3+R3 controller recovery chord remains available. [Full navigation controls](navigation.md#destination-controls-in-the-development-build)

## Course and native validity

- [The route worker](../src/world/route-worker.cjs) encodes one byte per verified tile before applying the eight-leg speech limit. Two bits encode the native direction and the upper six carry the capped distance to the next turn. A transfer hands at most 16,383 bytes to the renderer. The renderer advances one position only when the native input attempt succeeds in the expected direction and reaches the expected tile. A different step or an observed relocation retires the old cue and requests new directions. Scripted event progression keeps its native ownership.
- [The field adapter](../src/world/field.js) arms the beacon only from an admitted static worker result for the current map and target. The safe warning-sign approach in the cave keeps its authored area and rechecks patrol separation before each pulse. Timed cave previews retain their separate timing admission. [Cave route and patrol limits](puzzles-and-secrets.md#cave-review-and-the-warning-sign)
- [The beacon owner](../src/world/beacon.js) uses the existing native map update to request a rendered sample only when a pulse is due and the player is settled. The sample verifies the live target, map, next native `canPass` edge and actual facing. A relevant native blocker or player pose change revokes pending audio before a new pulse. Turn or movement validity is checked on every pulse and after the corresponding native notifications. The cadence is 14, 20 or 26 simulation updates, based on distance to the next turn. A pulse expires after 12 updates. The worker gets the cue, bearing, gain and generation through the shared audio service. An overlapping wall cue has priority. Cold preparation sends no PCM, then waits for a fresh usable sample.
- At the last tile, the adapter checks the installed `Game_Player.findEventsAt` and `canTriggerEvent` methods. It respects native on-tile priority, blocking front interactions, counter look-ahead and boarding priority. It uses the actual facing before announcing arrival. It reads eligibility without running the interaction. `rmmz_objects.js` source checks locate the player-facing assignment at original byte 189047 and the accepted step before position assignment at 195557. The native post-attempt marker is at 222850. The accepted event marker at 249454 revokes field audio before event lock. [Audio device and cancellation](audio.md#ownership-and-cancellation)
- For a parked surface craft, the beacon validates the same live craft, position and native boarding priority at the arrival approach. A floor-trigger dock uses the native movement route, and accepted vehicle events take control before new audio. The parked boat fixture verified an arrival cue and ordinary native boarding. [Vehicle handback and test limits](navigation.md#native-vehicle-transitions)
- Aircraft boarding uses the parked craft's exact tile. The flight beacon uses the same verified flight course as speech and rechecks the actual native landing rule before the arrival pair. The native fixture rejected a new occupant at the intended landing and verified the arrival cue after the tile cleared. [Airship controls and evidence](navigation.md#airship-landing-trace)

## Evidence and final gameplay checks

The installed-method fixture followed eight native steps to the Map 196 warning sign. It verified course continuation past a truncated spoken route, a rear-relative turn, the exact arrival approach, a deviating step, a new blocker, changed destination, and a closed field before the next render. The capped fixture measured 0.70 ms for the equivalent eight-step native traversal and 3.11 ms with silent beacon requests. Those totals cover extracted methods and synthetic output, with no physical hardware or in-game performance attribution. [Installed-method fixture](../tests/world.test.cjs)

The matching hidden NW.js test verified persistent controls, live route-worker handoff, the stereo PCM publication and cancellation in field settings. With 40 ms fixture polling, the warm native submission was observed after about 40 ms. The latest cold HRTF status appeared after 597 ms, and the build fixture rendered five warm cues in about 10 ms. Audible onset remains unmeasured. The C++ fixture verifies all three cue identities and stereo rear pitch. [Runtime fixture](../tests/nw-smoke.py) · [Native audio fixture](../tests/spatial-audio-fixture.cpp)

At final gameplay handoff, select a nearby destination that requires one turn. Request directions and hear both the spoken route and the first beacon pulse. Follow the native movement until the double turn cue, then hear the arrival pair at the approach. Request another route, deviate or stop it with the configured command, and listen for any stale pulse. Repeat with HRTF off, then try the cave warning sign when accessible. Keep audible timing, overlapping native audio, recognition and device-level transition quiescence open until those checks run.
