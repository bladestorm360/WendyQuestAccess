# Spatial audio

Open mod settings with **F8** on the default keyboard binding, choose **Audio**, then choose **Preview ahead**, **left**, **right**, **behind** or **above**. The settings dialog also accepts its configured controller navigation and confirm/back actions. **Mod sounds** and **HRTF** are independent and both default on. They persist in the NW profile. Turning sounds off prevents cues and previews and retains the HRTF preference. Returning to the root focuses **Audio** again. The separate speech master and soundscape remain open. [Route beacon](beacon.md) · [Live cave cues](puzzles-and-secrets.md#live-crossing-cues) · [Feature register](feature-checkpoint.md)

The preview status reports the output mode selected for the cue. A request that loses its dialog or scene drops its status. The fallback pans left and right relative to the listener and lowers pitch to 72% of the forward pitch across the rear half-plane. HRTF uses the same original cue with a three-dimensional bearing and the built-in OpenAL Soft dataset. The listener's axes are right, up and forward. The bridge reverses the forward axis for OpenAL's default listener orientation. Compare front, behind and above with headphones during final gameplay testing. Native game audio is owned by the game.

## Ownership and cancellation

- [The renderer service](../src/runtime/audio.js) starts a single Node worker on demand after the owning dialog passes its readiness checks. One request may be in flight and one newer request may wait. Each request sends a generation, three bearing components, a gain and the HRTF selection. Scene objects and readiness predicates stay on the renderer. Replacing a preview revokes its predecessor. A worker error closes admission and reports the output failure to the current dialog.
- [The plugin](../src/plugin.js) admits previews only for the focused, presented Audio dialog in the current scene. Group exit, sound/HRTF changes, settings retirement, native scene departure, resize, blur and page hide revoke admission before replacement work. The renderer only stores the closed generation. It never joins the worker or waits for the audio device.
- [The Windows addon](../src/services/windows/audio-addon.cpp) binds the narrow Node-API exports in the loaded NW `node.dll` and owns the SDK objects on the Node worker. It keeps a strong Buffer reference to the shared 32-bit admission word until the SDL stream has been destroyed. SDK entry points use application-relative absolute paths and constrained DLL lookup. The service declines a preloaded SDL3 or OpenAL Soft module of the same name. The DLLs remain separate from the addon. [Node 14 Node-API](https://nodejs.org/download/release/v14.12.0/docs/api/n-api.html#n_api_napi_get_buffer_info)
- [OpenAL Soft loopback](../src/services/windows/audio-hrtf.hpp) renders 48 kHz stereo into a bounded array on the worker, checking admission between 128-frame slices. It requires the runtime to report HRTF enabled and select its exact built-in dataset. The [cue bank](../src/services/windows/spatial-cue.hpp) uses short original tones with distinct envelopes and harmonics for the generic preview, wall bump and wall brush. Both output modes use the same definitions.
- [The SDL stream](../src/services/windows/audio-device.hpp) is the one hardware output for this service. The callback checks the shared generation and copies at most 1,024 source frames in subchunks of at most 256, checking again before each submission. SDL holds the recursive stream lock during the callback. The callback removes itself on completion or revocation so the idle stream has no recurring mod callback. The worker publishes completed PCM and owns stream destruction. [SDL callback locking](https://wiki.libsdl.org/SDL3/SDL_AudioStreamCallback) · [Callback removal](https://wiki.libsdl.org/SDL3/SDL_SetAudioStreamGetCallback) · [Recursive mutex semantics](https://wiki.libsdl.org/SDL3/SDL_LockMutex)

## Wall feedback

In **Audio**, turn **Wall sounds** on. It defaults off. Wall volume starts at 65 percent and can be raised or lowered by ten percentage points. **Preview wall bump ahead** and **Preview wall brush on the left** use the same gain, HRTF choice and cue definitions as field audio. Previews are available while wall sounds are off if the sound master is on and volume is above zero. Volume and wall preferences persist independently of the master.

- A rejected normal walking step can play a short bump in the attempted direction, relative to the player's native facing. A successful step can play a softer brush when the same lateral tile edge is blocked at both ends of the step. A corridor with two qualifying edges alternates sides on successive eligible cues. The brush uses live tile passage and the map boundary. Occupied actors and custom rectangles are handled by the actual rejected movement path. The service reads passage without issuing any movement or interaction.
- The game normally admits walking through `Game_Player.moveByInput`, which uses the accepted native cardinal direction from `Input.dir4` or the native pointer path. The installed enabled plugins were checked for overrides. `MapScene` changes collision and event touch while leaving this command path in place. `tools/registration.py` marks `rmmz_objects.js` at original byte 222850 after the attempt and at 249454 before accepted `Game_Event.start` sets its starting flag or locks the actor. The event marker cancels pending audio and blocks a bump from the same update. This preserves the original cave patrol contact and encounter. Scripted steps bypass the input observation. Menus, loading, active map events and vehicle movement stay outside the walking consumer. [Native gate and world evidence](navigation.md)
- [The wall owner](../src/world/wall-audio.js) makes at most four side-edge checks per accepted step, with a bound of 32 active tile events. An audit of all 239 map files found at most two authored tile-image events on one map. Cue pacing permits one every seven simulation updates at most and one every 18 updates for the same contact direction and cue. A move, facing change or field boundary can revoke a pending cue. The age limit is 12 updates. Preparation starts only in a ready field context and sends no PCM. Cold contact does not queue a stale cue for playback after initialization. A warm audio service is reused when the next settled field admits it.

## Offline evidence

November 11, 2026, Steam build 26578620. The bounded 512 MB build with `-VerifyAudio` passed the native fixture with the shipped SDL3 3.4.4 and OpenAL Soft 1.25.2 x64 libraries. It checked left/right channel energy, different front/rear/above HRTF outputs, rear pitch across the full rear half-plane and the callback's sample submission after replacement and revocation. The native fixture opened loopback in about 285–310 ms and rendered five warm cues in about 10–12 ms under the runner's 8.33% CPU cap. The isolated NW test used the matching 0.48.4 runtime and Node 14.12.0, a synthetic window, the production addon and the silent SDL driver. It verified HRTF preview, explicit stereo preview, the sounds-off switch, dialog focus and both saved settings after restart. Cold HRTF status appeared after about 505 ms with 40 ms polling. The [renderer fixture](../tests/audio.test.cjs) verifies early revocation, queue replacement, stale receipts and worker loss.

The wall fixture exercised extracted installed input, collision, passage and event start against the actual Salt Caverns and Map 196 data. It verified cold contact suppression, the bump and left brush bearings, repeat pacing, modal and age retirement, and an active cue revoked before the patrol's native event lock. The two runs measured 2.54–3.18 ms baseline and 2.79–3.10 ms with silent wall requests across 120 blocked native attempts. Those overlapping totals show the scale of work in this capped fixture. The native SDL dummy fixture verified that preparation is idle and that callback counts stop after both revocation and natural completion. The standalone NW test verified the packaged wall brush preview and settings persistence. Warm preview status appeared after about 40 ms with 40 ms polling. [Installed-method test](../tests/world.test.cjs) · [Device test](../tests/spatial-audio-fixture.cpp)

These measurements end at generated PCM, silent submission or UI status. Actual audible onset, hardware buffering, direction recognition, coexistence with native audio and comparable native application overhead remain unverified. The callback removes itself at the first opportunity after revocation. SDK calls already entered and PCM already buffered in SDL or the device may span the boundary. Device-level phase quiescence remains open. The cave's cue deadlines are verified through silent submission. Compare warm audible latency and cancellation against the native crossing window in the final gameplay pass. [Cave timing evidence](puzzles-and-secrets.md#live-crossing-cues)

At final gameplay handoff, first compare the two wall previews with headphones. On an ordinary walking map with a continuous tile wall, turn Wall sounds on and attempt one step into it. Expect a brief bump ahead. Walk alongside the edge. Expect a softer cue from the wall's side. A held blocked direction should repeat at a controlled cadence. Open a menu or transfer and listen for a lingering cue. Later in the cave, a patrol contact should follow the native encounter feedback without a competing wall bump. Missing, reversed or delayed cues and audio after a transition are failures to record.

## Dependencies and payload check

The builder uses the approved FFPR SDK inputs by default and accepts `-AudioSdkRoot` for an equivalent local source. It verifies the key input hashes before staging the two runtime libraries and their notices in `audio-deps`. OpenAL Soft includes its MIT KEMAR measurements in the DLL. The matching corresponding-source URL and measurement attribution travel in `audio-deps/SOURCE.txt`. The Windows release must preserve those files and the SDL license. The current platform target is Windows x64. [SDL 3.4.4](https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL/releases/tag/release-3.4.4) · [OpenAL Soft 1.25.2 HRTF](https://github.com/kcat/openal-soft/blob/1.25.2/docs/hrtf.txt) · [MIT KEMAR measurements and terms](https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR.html)

Static PE inspection confirmed x64 for the addon and both audio SDKs. The addon exports `napi_register_module_v1` and imports `KERNEL32.dll`. The SDKs' static imports are Windows system libraries. Microsoft Defender custom-scanned the extracted cave progression development payload on November 12, 2026 at 07:39 UTC and reported no threats. Platform 4.18.26100.4, engine 1.1.26100.4, definitions 1.463.257.0 updated at 05:18 UTC. The native files reported `NotSigned`. The final release still needs its own exact payload and installer checks, signing status and installed verification.
