# WendyQuest accessibility mod

Launch WendyQuest through Steam. Press **F8** to open accessibility settings. Use the **arrow keys**, **Enter** and **Escape** to navigate. **Help** has sections for menus, the pathfinder, battle and controller controls.

## Field controls

- Use the game’s arrow keys or WASD to move. Use Enter or Space to interact when the game shows its prompt.
- In the default Letters layout, press **J** or **L** to browse destinations. Use **Shift+J** or **Shift+L** to change category, **K** to read the selection and **P** for route instructions. Destinations are ordered by proximity, with your selection kept as you move.
- **All** combines available destinations and saved bookmarks. **NPC** lists available characters. **Shift+P** (Letters) or **Shift+Backslash** (Brackets and backslash) switches between All destinations and Reachable only. You can also change the filter in **Navigation**. In Salt Caverns, **Shift+G** gives the next puzzle step and directions to its approach. **Events** gives directions to reachable scene triggers. Follow a marked trigger from **Quests**. Books, barrels and treasure use type labels. The French Fry Forest gate switches are in **Objects**. Press the action button at a switch. If a Crispy Beach route stops at a floor event, take the final step manually and request directions again after control returns. Save and load bookmarks under **Navigation → Bookmarks**.
- Press **M** for the current map name, **Shift+M** for a short description of the terrain, visible landmarks and weather where applicable, or **Q** for the visible quest objective and count. The mod also announces named maps as you enter them.
- Press **R** to repeat dialogue, **I** to read available details and **Shift+G** for supported puzzle guidance. Use **World Map** from Items and press **I** for the quest pin, visible objective and nearby atlas landmarks.
- **F8 → Audio** has separate switches for **Navigation beacon**, **Wall sounds**, and **Footstep sounds**. The beacon pans toward the next movement key and speeds up from 1.5 seconds to 0.3 seconds between pings. Three rising notes confirm arrival. Each blocked direction plays its own wall tone at the same time. The four pitches follow the movement keys even when Wendy turns. After three seconds without a step, the tone fades over half a second. Each footstep is a dry heel-to-toe pair of pips.
- Press **H** for Wendy’s visible status on the field, in pause and Inventory, or in battle. This includes HP, FP and the other gauges or conditions that are shown. On the field, HP and FP are read as percentages. H includes “critical” while the field low-health warning is active. Press **G** to read the gold shown in the pause menu or shop.
- On a default Xbox-style controller, use the **right stick** to browse and **left trigger** for directions. Press and release **View**, then press **A** to read the selected destination or **Menu** for settings.

Enable **Cutscene descriptions** in **F8 → Audio** to hear short descriptions of selected story animations in a separate voice. Adjust its volume and speed in the same menu. The default speed is 200 percent.

## Battle

At the start of battle, the mod reads the enemy formation before the first focused command. Highlight a target to hear its visible HP percentage. Press **I** to hear appearance, current HP and conditions. With battle commands open, **I** describes the active fighter’s sprite and visible status effects. On a companion ability, **I** reads the ability and **Shift+I** describes the companion. Appearance is read only when requested. Press **I** on a dialogue line to describe the active speaker when there is a verified portrait or sprite. Map diagrams still use **I** for their details.

Confirm to start the timing meter. Confirm again for power, then for accuracy. **F8 → Audio → Battle timing assist** adds one note when a perfect window opens. Press Confirm when you hear it. The game’s wind-up sounds provide the rest of the timing. When the game shows its resistance popup, speech reports that Wendy resisted a status effect.
