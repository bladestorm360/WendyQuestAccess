# WendyQuest accessibility mod

Launch WendyQuest through Steam. Press **F8** to open accessibility settings. Use the **arrow keys**, **Enter** and **Escape** to navigate. **Help** has sections for menus, the pathfinder, battle and controller controls.

## Field controls

- Use the game’s arrow keys or WASD to move. Use Enter or Space to interact when the game shows its prompt.
- In the default Letters layout, press **J** or **L** to browse destinations. Use **Shift+J** or **Shift+L** to change category, **K** to read the selection and **P** for route instructions. Destinations are ordered by proximity, with your selection kept as you move.
- **All** combines available destinations and saved bookmarks. **NPC** lists available characters. **Events** gives directions to reachable scene triggers. Follow a marked trigger from **Quests**. Books, barrels and treasure use type labels. If a Crispy Beach route stops at a floor event, take the final step manually and request directions again after control returns. Save and load bookmarks under **Navigation → Bookmarks**.
- Press **M** for the current map name or **Q** for the visible quest objective and count. The mod also announces named maps as you enter them.
- Press **R** to repeat dialogue, **I** to read available details and **Shift+G** for supported puzzle guidance.
- **F8 → Audio** has separate switches for **Navigation beacon**, **Wall sounds**, and **Footstep sounds**. The beacon refreshes as you walk. The four wall pitches follow the movement keys even when Wendy turns. After three seconds without a step, the tone fades over half a second. Each footstep is a dry heel-to-toe pair of pips.
- Press **H** for Wendy’s HP. The field reports the bar percentage. The pause menu reports the exact values. Press **G** to read the gold shown in the pause menu or shop.
- On a default Xbox-style controller, use the **right stick** to browse and **left trigger** for directions. Press and release **View**, then press **A** to read the selected destination or **Menu** for settings.

## Battle

Confirm to start the timing meter. Confirm again for power, then for accuracy. **F8 → Audio → Battle timing assist** adds one note when a perfect window opens. Press Confirm when you hear it. The game’s wind-up sounds provide the rest of the timing.
